    2. Open a save file from the desktop app

    Your profiles live in C:\Invenfloor\data\ while you run from source, or %APPDATA%\InventoryApp\data\ once it is packaged. Pick any .json in there. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the file is read in this page and thrown away when you close the tab.

    3. Does the saving work?

    The same checks storage_run.mjs runs in node, run here against the real IndexedDB instead of a stand-in. They use databases of their own, wiped before and after, so your own profiles in invenfloor are never touched.

    The saving checks run twice, once plain and once with a passphrase over the database. Not two sets of checks, the same set. That is the claim encryption has to earn: it changes nothing.

            5. What is saved in this browser

            The real store, the one the app will use. It belongs to this browser on this device: what you save in Chrome here is not what Safari on a phone will show, because there is no server yet. Safari clears this after a week of not visiting. Which is why there is a Download button, and why the app will push it.
